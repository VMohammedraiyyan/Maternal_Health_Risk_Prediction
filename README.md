# Maternal_Health_Risk_Prediction
PREDICTIVE MODELLING OF MATERNAL HEALTH RISK USING MACHINE LEARNING APPROACHES
Abstract
**Background:** Maternal health is a critical area in healthcare, as complications during pregnancy can directly impact the well-being of both the mother and child. Early identification of risk factors plays a vital role in preventing adverse outcomes. In this study, the Maternal Health Risk Assessment Dataset (MHRAD) is used to build predictive models for classifying maternal health risk levels. This dataset comprises detailed clinical, physiological, and historical health information collected from maternal patients to evaluate potential health risks during pregnancy.  
**Objective:** The main objective of this research is to develop and evaluate machine learning models that can accurately predict maternal health risk levels. By leveraging patient health data, the study seeks to support healthcare professionals in identifying high-risk cases early, thereby improving maternal care and reducing preventable complications. 
**Methods:** Supervised Machine Learning models such as Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest and Support Vector Machine were trained and tested on the dataset. The predictive performance of the models was evaluated using multiple metrics, including accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score.  To facilitate user interaction, a Graphical User Interface (GUI) was developed using the Tkinter library in Python.
**Results:** Logistic Regression achieved an accuracy of 97% with precision, recall and F1-scores of 99%, 96% and 97% respectively. The Random Forest model also attained an accuracy of 97%, with precision, recall and F1-scores of 100%, 96% and 89% respectively. The Decision Tree classifier performed comparatively well with 96% accuracy and balanced precision and recall values, while the Support vector classifier showed relatively lower performance compared to the other models.
**Conclusion:** This study demonstrates that machine learning can serve as an effective tool for predicting maternal health risks. By enabling early and reliable detection of high-risk cases, such approaches can empower clinicians to provide timely interventions.
